<Meta title="Design System/Motion" />

<Title />

Motion explains change, continuity, and spatial relationships. It must not exist only to decorate an idle interface.

- Use transitions when they help users understand where content appeared, moved, or disappeared.
- Keep frequently repeated control feedback immediate.
- Do not make essential state or information available only through animation.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences when motion is not required for comprehension.
